Why Olympus

Built for speed, designed for precision. Every feature crafted for seamless translation workflow.

Instant Hotkey Translation

Select text in any application, press your custom shortcut, and Olympus copies, translates, and pastes the result back — all in under a second. No context switching, no extra clicks.

Voice-to-Text Translation

Hold the voice hotkey, speak naturally, and watch your words appear as translated text at your cursor. Powered by Whisper for 99+ languages with zero setup.

Neural AI Enhancement

Beyond literal translation. Olympus uses Groq, Claude, or GPT to fix grammar, adapt tone, and make your text sound native — whether it's formal docs or casual chat.

Privacy First

All translations happen locally. Your text never leaves your machine unless you choose a cloud AI provider.

100+ Languages

Translate between any of 100+ languages with automatic detection. Cyrillic, CJK, Arabic — all supported.

Custom Themes

Switch between light, dark, and AMOLED themes. Adjust fonts, colors, and layout to match your workflow.

Multi-Provider AI

Switch between Groq, Claude, GPT, and HuggingFace. Use free tiers or your own API keys.
