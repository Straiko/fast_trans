Features

Everything you need

A single tool that replaces switching between translator tabs, copy-paste juggling, and manual voice transcription.

Global Hotkeys

Select text anywhere, press your shortcut. Olympus captures, translates, and auto-pastes the result back.

Voice Input

Hold your voice hotkey, speak, release. Speech is recognized, translated, and pasted at cursor position.

AI Enhancement

LLMs fix speech errors, improve grammar, and make translations sound natural in the target language.

Auto Replace

Translation replaces selected text in-place. No clipboard juggling, no switching windows.

Customizable

Pick hotkeys, source and target languages, AI provider, model, microphone device. Full control in settings.

Local LLM Support

Run Ollama locally for fully private, offline AI translation. No API keys, no data leaves your machine.

Workflow

Three steps. Zero friction.

1

Select

Highlight text in any application or hold your voice hotkey and speak into your mic.

2

Translate

Press your hotkey. Olympus grabs the text, routes it through the translation engine and AI optimizer.

3

Done

The translated text auto-pastes back at your cursor. Whole workflow under one second.
